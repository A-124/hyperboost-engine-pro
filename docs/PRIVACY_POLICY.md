# Privacy Policy — HyperBoost Engine Pro

**Last updated: October 28, 2026**

HyperBoost Engine Pro ("the App") is an Android gaming utility maintained by A-124. This policy explains what the App handles, why, and the choices you have.

## Information the App handles

- **Device and system information** needed for the features you use, such as Android version, device model, display and performance information, and settings you choose to view or change. Availability depends on your device and permissions.
- **Game profiles and preferences**, stored locally on your device so the App can remember your choices.
- **Optional feedback**: the category, your message, app version, Android version, device model, submission time and a `new` status. Feedback also uses an anonymous Firebase user ID. No name, email address, account or contact details are requested.
- **Optional capability report**: off by default and enabled by you. When enabled, it contains a normalized operation signature (for example `settings put system`), whether your device accepted or rejected it, the Android API level and app version. It never contains setting values, package names, device identifiers or messages, and it is not linked to feedback submissions or to any account.

## How information is used

- Local profiles and preferences are used only to provide the features you choose.
- Feedback is used to diagnose issues and improve the App.
- Capability reports are used to see which operations a given Android version accepts, so unsupported controls can be reported instead of failing.

## Sharing

- **Google Firebase** provides anonymous sign-in and Firestore storage for feedback, and now also stores capability reports when you enable them.
- **GitHub** hosts the optional feedback fallback. Feedback sent through **Send on GitHub** becomes a public GitHub issue; it is governed by GitHub's privacy policy, not by this one.
- **Shizuku** is a separate third-party app; its own privacy practices apply when you install and authorize it.
- The developer does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed if required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the App.

## Retention and deletion

- Local profiles and preferences can be removed by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.
- Feedback is retained until it has been reviewed and is no longer needed. To request deletion, open an issue in the project repository and include the approximate submission time; do not post private information.
- Capability reports are retained until they have been reviewed and are no longer needed. Disabling the report stops further submissions; to request deletion of earlier reports, open an issue in the project repository.

## Permissions and control

Android may request permissions or special access for particular features, including Shizuku authorization, overlay access, accessibility, notifications and installing app updates. Grant only the access needed for the features you use. You can revoke permissions in Android Settings; some features will stop working if the required access is removed. The capability report is off by default and can be turned off again at any time from the feedback dialog.

## Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed at children. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service.

## Security

Use only official releases from the project repository. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and device-level security depends in part on your Android configuration and the third-party tools you authorize.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. The latest version is published at this URL.
