Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers before you install.

Does HyperBoost Engine Pro require root?

It is designed as a rootless utility. Some features require Shizuku and the relevant Android permissions. Compatibility depends on your device and software.

How can HyperBoost help with my gaming setup?

HyperBoost puts supported tuning controls, live performance information, display and refresh-rate options, per-game preferences, and floating gaming tools in one place. Use the options available on your device to personalize your setup and see how it behaves in your games.

Why do some features show as unavailable?

Android manufacturers expose and restrict different system functions. Make sure required permissions and Shizuku are configured, then check whether your device supports the option.

What data does the app handle?

Profiles and settings stay on your device. If you choose to send feedback, it includes your message, app version, Android version and device model, stored through Firebase. No account or contact details are required. See the privacy policy.

Where should I download the APK?

Use the official GitHub latest release page. Compare the APK with SHA256SUMS.txt, avoid unofficial mirrors, and review the release notes before installing.

Where can I report a bug?

Open an issue on the project repository and include your app version, Android version, device model and steps to reproduce. Do not include passwords or private tokens.
